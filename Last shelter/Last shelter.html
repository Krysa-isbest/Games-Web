DAY 107:00

LAST SHELTER

The surface belongs to the dead
  • Click rock next to a dug chamber to dig. Shovels and pickaxes are safe. Bare hands are slow, hurt, and can make you sick.
  • Click an empty chamber to build a room. Every room costs supplies and needs a worker to build it.
  • Quick orders: click a survivor, then right-click a room, a build site or rock to send them there.
  • At first you can only build the basics: Well, Mushroom Cave, Bedroom, Storage and Workshop. Each Workshop level unlocks new rooms and new things to craft.
  • Survivors eat and drink by themselves when a full portion fits (food meter at 55, water at 65), so nothing is wasted. Run out of supplies and they start dying. A 🥫 / 💧 bubble above someone means they're going hungry or thirsty. Click it to feed them by hand.
  • Digging through earth turns up 🪵 wood (roots and old beams). Hard rock gives scrap.
  • Pick a difficulty when you start. You can change the meters later in Settings.
  • Multiplayer: up to 3 players share one bunker. Big decisions (digging, building, crafting, scavenging, event choices) need everyone to agree: F1 = yes, F2 = no.
  • Every room has upgrades. Power runs from generators and dynamos. Hidden pockets in the rock can hold supplies, tools, or other survivors.
  • At night the dead come for the hatch. Repair it, upgrade it, and staff an armory.
  • With 2 survivors you can send someone to scavenge. Pick a place on the city map (each has its own dangers, loot and travel time), pick who goes, then gear them up.
  • Scroll or drag to move between floors and the surface. Save in Settings. If everyone dies, the save is gone.
