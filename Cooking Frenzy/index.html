    ☀️ A day in the kitchen

    Prep first: cut things, stock up, plan. Flip the OPEN sign by the pass when you are ready (it opens by itself after 90 seconds). Service lasts 8 minutes, then there is one last round to finish the orders.

    The goals panel shows three goals for the day with cash rewards. New chefs get a tutorial: follow the yellow arrow through your first burger.

    Money carries over. At the end of each day you can buy upgrades and new dishes, and spend your skill points.

    🥕 The pantry

    Every ingredient has its own bin: produce crates on the long counter, buns and dough in the baskets above, meat and cheese in the deli case. Click a bin or press E on it to take one. Fresh, whole ones go back with E (or just drop them into their bin).

    Running low? Order more at the terminal by the back door: the truck puts the food straight into the bins. You get a small free delivery every morning.

    🔪 Cooking

    Put food on a cutting board and press E to cut it. Fry in a pan on a burner (E on the knob sets the heat), then press E on the pan to flip. Fries go in the fryer basket, pizza in the oven with the door closed. Soup: fill a pot at the sink, add 3 tomatoes and an onion, boil, stir and salt it.

    Drop food onto a plate or into a bowl to plate it. The order you stack a burger in matters!

    🛎️ Serving

    Put finished dishes on the pass and ring the bell: the runner takes them to the right table. Or carry a plate out yourself and press E on the guest for a bigger tip. Greet waiting guests (E) and they wait longer.

    You can hand a guest anything, but they judge it against their order: the wrong dish, missing parts or food without a plate cost stars and pay.

    Clear finished tables before the busser does (he takes 25 seconds). Dirty dishes go to the red tray by the sink: wash them (E at the sink) or buy more.

    🔥 Chaos

    Anything that touches the floor is dirty. Plates break when you throw them. Burnt food catches fire and fire spreads: grab a red extinguisher and hold F. A big fire that burns too long gets the kitchen shut down.

    🎲 Rush events

    🎩 Food critic (triple pay at 4★) · 🏃 Lunch rush · 🐀 Rat raid (throw things at it!) · 📋 Health inspector · 🛢️ Grease spill · 🔦 Blackout.

    ⭐ Every job you do earns EXP. Level up for skill points: kitchen and service skills, all of them always work.
