What's new

v0.6

  • Deck guns: you look straight down the barrel with a gunsight, and the mouse moves the gun. RMB zooms, a ring shows where the shot lands.
  • Five deck gun types: deck gun, autocannon, mortar, rocket pod and torpedo tube. In the Garage, click a gun mount on your ship to change it.
  • A new Garage: your ship turns on the water (drag it), with readable panels for ship, armament, upgrades, loadout, skills and paint. Settings are new and easier to read too.
  • New driving: hold W/S to move the throttle (it stops at STOP first), A/D move the rudder. Ships have momentum, slide in turns and need water past the rudder. R drops the anchor.
  • An engine room in the stern hold. If it gets hit, the ship can't move until someone fixes it with the wrench.
  • Tools you always carry: 5 bucket, 6 hammer (puts single blocks back, free but slow), 7 wrench.
  • The crane is at the stern now, with its own controls: walk up to it, no need to drive.
  • Ladders work: walk into one from the water to climb, or climb down from the deck. Jumping works every time.
  • Smaller blocks (0.2 m), redesigned ships (bridge wings, hull windows, a raised bow, a towing arch) and furniture in the rooms: bunks, lockers, sofas, shelves, workbenches.
  • Flood water in the hold sloshes and ripples, and you wade through it.
  • Campaign: after every fight the dock gives 2 free planks and a repair for 10 FT. Harder difficulties pay much more FT.
  • You keep picked-up weapons, airstrikes and nukes when you die. Online players no longer spawn inside walls.
  • Main menu music (with its own volume slider), random cannon sounds and a C4 sound.

v0.5

  • Real ship collisions: hulls collide by their shape, heavy ships shove light ones aside, and damage is torn out where they touch (bows are tougher). Hit an island and you run aground: you have to back off.
  • Hold S at the helm to brake hard, stop and go into reverse. Reverse is now half of full speed.
  • A much bigger nuke: flash, fireball, mushroom cloud, a shockwave over the water, a blast wave that shoves ships, an orange sky and falling ash.
  • Enemy ships try to keep up with you, but sloppily, and hold course when you get close, so you can board them. Some small ones try to ram you.
  • Holding a plank shows a green preview of what gets fixed. Leaks below the waterline glow red, and the ship panel shows which hold is leaking.
  • Buckets: filling takes a moment, running or jumping spills water, and the water flies in an arc. Over the side it's gone, on a fire it puts it out, on the deck half of it runs back into the hold.
  • Wider, gentler stairs to the hold, with a railing, a lamp and a sign.
  • Fixed: leaving a deck gun put you inside a wall, and the aiming camera only showed the wall.
  • Your own sounds and music: put files in the audio folder (see audio/README.txt).

v0.4

  • Online play for up to 4: FFA (with AI captains to fill up), a co-op campaign where the crew votes on the route, and a shared sandbox. Room codes or LAN codes, voice chat (proximity) and text chat (Enter).
  • Every ship type now has its own design: tug fenders, yacht decks, trawler gantry, ferry twin funnels and ramp, a container ship bridge tower with cranes, a six-deck cruise ship with a pool and waterslide.
  • Fixed: you could only take the helm or use deck guns while standing at the plank storage.
  • Sharks now patrol around the ships with big fins and a foam trail.
  • Chase camera at the helm (X).

v0.3

  • Modern ships: tugboat, motor yacht, fishing trawler, ferry, container ship and cruise ship. They come unarmed: buy deck guns in the Garage.
  • Real underwater look: wobbly light, god rays, murk, floating particles and bubbles. Flooded holds are murky too.
  • A brand new menu, advanced graphics settings, voice chat settings with a mic test and rebindable keys.
  • Fixed: A and D strafed the wrong way.

v0.2

  • 0.25 m voxels, new ocean, new guns with arms and attachments, Garage with levels and FT, fires, FFA and the campaign.

🧭 Campaign

A route of 7 stops: battles, wrecks, storms, shops and calm coves, then the Ferryman's ghost liner. Damage stays between stops; after every fight the dock gives you 2 planks and a 10 FT repair. If your ship sinks, the run is over. Harder runs pay much more FT.
