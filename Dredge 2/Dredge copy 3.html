Controls & Seamanship

How to keep a crew alive out there.
The Helmsman takes the wheel (stand behind it and press Interact) and steers in third person. Dock beside a harbor pier while moving slowly.
The Lookout scans the fog with the spyglass (hold Aim) to mark monsters for the whole crew, patches leaks with the hammer, and fires flares to drive monsters away.
Bailing: with the bucket, press Use to scoop water off the deck, then carry it to the rail, look out over the side and press Use again to throw it into the sea. Throw it anywhere else and it just splashes back onto the deck.
The Galley: once the cabin is refitted, a blender and a recipe book stand on the counter inside. Blend two or three fruits from the hold into a smoothie that boosts the whole crew. Berries and fruit grow on landing islands and are sold at harbor markets.
Sea events: hurricanes, waterspouts, whirlpools and rogue waves (turn your bow into them!) threaten the boat. Fish frenzies and the aurora bring rare catches.
Rods: there are nine. Each better rod casts farther, reels faster, has a wider catch band and tires fish out sooner, so even legendary fish and leviathans fight less.
Landings (green on the chart) are jetties on uninhabited islands: moor there at the helm, then press Interact on deck to step ashore and explore. The boat can't cast off while anyone is still ashore. Berry bushes grow there — hold Interact to pick, sell the baskets at any harbor. Moonberries only ripen at night.
Your ship changes as you upgrade it. A Cabin Refit opens the wheelhouse door: sleep in a bunk in the evening while moored or anchored to skip to dawn. Shipwrights also sell the larger Albatross.
Monsters: flares drive most of them off. Hold the searchlight on a Phantom Ship to banish it; outrun or flare a Razorgill swarm; keep moving when a Bonewhale shadows you, or be ready to bail.
Fishing: hold Use to charge a cast toward bubbling water, wait for the bobber to dip, click to hook, then hold Use to keep the green band over the fish until the line is reeled in.
Rarer and larger fish pay more but fight harder. Night brings aberrations, monsters, and fortune.
