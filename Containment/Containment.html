CONTAINMENT

The chamber seals behind you. Drones pour out of the floor hatches in waves, a Warden arrives every fifth wave, and between waves you choose an upgrade. Supply pods drop in every wave (hold E beside one to hack it open) and gold Carrier drones hold rare parts. Press TAB for your inventory: enemies drop materials you can craft into shield rechargers, cells and kits. Visit the workbenches on the outer walls to install weapon attachments and robot upgrades. Your shield never regenerates on its own. Scrap from kills buys a Mystery Box roll, locked doors, an Armoury with wall-buy guns, a Med Bay and the Forge deep inside Vault 07. Hold the floor as long as you can. Aim at limbs: a drone with a leg shot off will hop or crawl, and one that loses an arm drops its weapon.

Reminders

Headshots do more than double damage, and armor plates on brutes and the Warden absorb most of it. Hazard-striped crates can be jumped onto, and the raised gantries are safe from melee drones. Shoot a limb to chip voxels off it: destroyed legs slow enemies to a crawl, and a destroyed gun arm disarms shooters. Shields only refill from rechargers you craft (TAB), shield cells and orbs. Jump or dash to avoid the Warden's slam. Slide by pressing crouch while sprinting, or dive with the prone key.
